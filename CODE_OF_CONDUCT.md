# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be respectful. Assume good faith. Criticise code, not people.

This project is maintained by one person in their own time. That shapes what
you can expect: responses may be slow, and a well-argued issue may still be
declined because it is not where the maintainer wants to spend the time. Both
are normal and neither is a slight.

## Standards

Examples of behaviour that helps:

- Giving concrete, actionable feedback, with a reproduction where relevant.
- Accepting a "no" without relitigating it.
- Assuming that a decision you disagree with had a reason, and asking what it
  was before assuming there wasn't one.

Examples of behaviour that does not:

- Personal attacks, insults, or demeaning comments.
- Harassment of any kind, public or private.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions,
commit messages, code review — and when representing the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to <joshstout@gmail.com>. Reports are handled
privately. The maintainer will do what seems proportionate, which may include
editing or removing content, or blocking an account.

There is no appeals committee. There is one maintainer, who will try to be
fair and will sometimes get it wrong.
